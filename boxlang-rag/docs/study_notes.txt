Introduction to Renewable Energy

Renewable energy comes from natural sources that are continuously replenished.

Examples of renewable energy include solar energy, wind energy, hydropower, geothermal energy, and biomass.

Solar panels produce electricity by converting sunlight into electrical energy.

Wind turbines generate electricity by using the movement of wind to turn a turbine.

Hydropower generates electricity from moving water.

Renewable energy can reduce dependence on fossil fuels and can help reduce greenhouse gas emissions.
